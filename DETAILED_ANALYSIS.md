## Key Takeaways for Product Video Strategy

Based on the macro-analysis of all 77 videos, here is the formula for creating viral, high-converting product demos in 2026:

1.  **Keep it Under 60 Seconds:** The vast majority of the top-performing videos (e.g., Computer Use, 77.9M views) run between 30 and 70 seconds. Get to the Magic Moment before the 15-second mark.
2.  **Show "Thinking" as a UI Element:** Don't just show the final output. Visualizing the *process*—via terminal outputs, node graphs, or step-by-step progress checklists—builds trust and perceived value.
3.  **The Split-Screen Paradigm:** Users love seeing the "Command" and the "Result" simultaneously. Whether it's code vs. preview, or prompt vs. spreadsheet, side-by-side visualization is the industry standard.
4.  **Emphasize Autonomy:** The highest engagement comes when the AI is shown breaking out of a strict Q&A loop. Show the AI navigating a UI, jumping between tasks, or spawning sub-tasks independently.
